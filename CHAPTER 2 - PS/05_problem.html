Mount Manaslu.

Located in the Mansiri Himal range in the west-central part of Nepal,
is the world's eighth-highest mountain at 8,163 meters (26,781 feet).
Famous for its sweeping glaciers and dramatic topography,
it attracts elite mountaineers for 8,000-meter peak expeditions and trekkers tackling the scenic yet challenging Manaslu Circuit.

*Overview of Mt. Manaslu:-


 Elevation: 8,163 meters (26,781 feet).

Location: Gorkha and Manang districts of Nepal,
             about 64 kilometers east of Annapurna.

Name Meaning: Derived from the Sanskrit word manasa,
             which translates to "intellect" or "soul".

First Ascent: Successfully summited on May 9, 1956,
             by Toshio Imanishi and Gyaltsen Norbu Sherpa from a Japanese expedition.

*Manaslu Expedition:-


Climbing Season:Typically late August through October (Autumn) or Spring.

Route:Expeditions usually climb the North-East Face,
             which involves moderately-angled snow slopes,
             glacier crossings, and technical crevasse navigation. 

Duration:The full climbing expedition often takes approximately 65 days from arrival to summit and return. 

Preparation: Known as a technical but rewarding climb,
             it is highly favored by mountaineers preparing to tackle Mount Everest or other higher altitude peaks.
